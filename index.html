Preserve. Connect. Remember.

More than a collection of records.

Patriot Legacy brings together the history of Allen County’s schools: athletics and honors, classrooms and the arts, everyday life and lasting traditions.

Each published record will connect its story to a source. As the archive grows, yearbook pages, photographs, documents, and interview transcripts will become part of the same searchable history.

Help preserve the next chapter.

We are building the archive before adding its collections. Historical school names, photographs, yearbooks, ephemera, and personal recollections all have a place here.
